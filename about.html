About Macway Biotech

Macway Biotech Private Limited supports healthcare professionals in their daily efforts to improve the lives of their patients. Based at Hyderabad, specialized in development and sales, we are professionally of high quality offering range of products from initial wound bed preparation through to full wound closure. With good exposure and experience in Neuro Psychiatry industry, which we believe can be put use in addressing the difficulties encountered in the management for treatment.

We do this by taking a pioneering approach to the design of our advanced medical products and services, by securing wider access to our diverse technologies for more customers globally, and by enabling better outcomes for patients and healthcare systems. We have good holding positions in Advanced Wound Management, Neuropathic Pain, Psychiatric Illness, Reconstruction and Fixation, Sports Medicine and Trauma Extremities.

Macway Biotech Private Limited launched a division Makrophage in India with focus on scientific innovation to the art of wound management. Our product & service portfolio includes a range of Advanced and Active wound care products concentrating in the niche segment of wound Care market of India along with Neuropathic Pain & Psychiatric Illness.

Offering a complete and full line of Healing products to meet the needs of health care professionals. With a focus on marketing of pharmaceutical products and services to the doctors & corporate clients across India.

Macway Biotech Private Limited is committed to helping people regain their lives by repairing and healing the human body. We also value our commitment to our employees, the medical fraternity, our distributors and dealers all of whom have played an important part in establishing Macway Biotech Private Limited as a fast growing pharma company.

Some of our products conform to CE Mark, ISO-9001-2008, ISO-13485-AC-2007 and WHO-GMP Certifications.
