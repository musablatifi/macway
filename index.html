About Macway Biotech

Healing with Science.
Advancing with Purpose.

Macway Biotech Private Limited is a Hyderabad-based specialty healthcare company dedicated to supporting healthcare professionals with reliable, clinically relevant and patient-focused solutions.

Our portfolio spans the complete healing journey—from wound-bed preparation and infection management to tissue repair and wound closure. We also bring focused expertise to neuropathic pain, neuropsychiatry, reconstruction and fixation, sports medicine, trauma and extremity care.

Focused on
Advanced Wound Management

Through Makrophage, our specialised wound-management division, we provide advanced and active wound-care solutions designed to address complex clinical needs across India.

By combining scientific understanding with practical clinical insight, we aim to make innovative wound-care technologies more accessible to healthcare professionals and help enable better outcomes for patients.

Innovation with Clinical Purpose

Specialty care,
built with purpose.

Our approach begins with understanding the real challenges faced by doctors, hospitals and patients. This understanding guides us in developing and marketing products that are relevant, dependable and easy to integrate into clinical practice.

Every solution in our portfolio is driven by a clear purpose: to support healing, simplify treatment and improve quality of life.
